From My Home to Yours

My love for holiday scents began long before The Holiday Candle. I remember cozy winter nights with my family — wrapping presents by the fire, the kitchen alive with sweet holiday baking, mugs of cocoa in our hands, and carols playing under the glow of twinkling lights.

Those moments of warmth, cheer, and nostalgia are what I wanted to share with others. That’s the heart behind The Holiday Candle. Each candle is hand-poured to capture the joy of the season, with fragrances inspired by festive desserts and holiday traditions, all pointing us back to Jesus, the true Light of the world.

From my hands to your home, thank you for letting The Holiday Candle be a part of your celebrations, gatherings, and cozy nights in
