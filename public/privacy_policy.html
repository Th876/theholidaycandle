Privacy Policy

The Holiday Candle values your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you provide when placing an order, subscribing to our newsletter, or contacting us. This may include:

  • Name
  • Shipping and billing address
  • Email address
  • Payment information (processed securely via Stripe)

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used for the following purposes:

  • To process and fulfill orders
  • To communicate order updates and customer support inquiries
  • To send marketing emails if you have opted in
  • To improve our website and services

3. Payment Security

All payments are processed via Stripe, a secure and trusted payment processor. We do not store your card information on our servers.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. Your information may be shared with:

  • Shipping carriers to deliver your order
  • Payment processors (Stripe) to process payments
  • Third-party service providers as needed for operational purposes, i.e. Stripe and USPS

5. Cookies & Tracking

Our site may use cookies to improve user experience and track website analytics. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

6. Your Rights

You may access, update, or request deletion of your personal information collected via marketing emails by contacting us at info@theholidaycandle.store

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with the updated effective date.
